## Cross-article controls

- Record the exact source version, publication date and access date for changing prices, model configs and specifications.
- Distinguish measured results, vendor claims, calculations, estimates and editorial interpretation.
- Do not call arithmetic vectors a hardware benchmark.
- Do not present a vendor rate card as a guaranteed quote.
- Preserve source excerpts and calculation inputs where lawful.
- Obtain a response from affected parties before publishing consequential allegations.
- Disclose Tinlance relationships and all material commercial interests.
- Add the article to the CMS only after the author, reviewer, fact-check and editor have completed the required workflow.
- Keep all eight items in draft state until human assignments and review records exist.
