# HEZQEN Public Governance Pages

These public pages form the reader-facing governance layer:

- `/about` — publication mission, ownership and editorial independence.
- `/editorial-standards` — evidence, authorship, AI boundaries, review and corrections.
- `/research-methodology` — research questions, source evaluation, claim verification, reproducibility and uncertainty.
- `/authors` — authorship, contributor consent, reviewer roles and conflicts of interest.
- `/commercial-disclosure` — Tinlance ownership, sponsorship, affiliate links and related-party coverage.
- `/corrections` — how readers report concerns and how material corrections are displayed.

The pages are linked from the site footer and mobile navigation and included in the dynamic XML sitemap. The policies describe the intended editorial controls; operational acceptance still requires actual human assignments, review records and approved publication workflows. No contributor identity or credential should be invented to populate a profile.
