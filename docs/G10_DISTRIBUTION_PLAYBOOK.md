# G10 — HEZQEN Distribution Playbook

**Status:** Playbook prepared; no campaign has been launched and no reach or conversion results are claimed.

## Positioning

HEZQEN is a global technology and business intelligence publication for engineers, security practitioners, founders and decision-makers. Its four divisions are AI & Technology, Cybersecurity, Cloud & Engineering, and Business & Startups.

Editorial promise: **Research. Intelligence. Perspective.**  
Every distribution asset must link to a substantive HEZQEN article, transparent methodology or useful calculator—not to an empty landing page.

## Distribution sequence

### 1. Owned channels

- Weekly HEZQEN Intelligence Brief after the double-opt-in email lifecycle is verified.
- Website research hubs and calculator landing pages.
- LinkedIn company page and founder distribution.
- X research threads and concise data-led posts.
- YouTube explainers and screen-recorded calculator walkthroughs.

### 2. Earned channels

- Hacker News and relevant technical subreddits only when the content genuinely answers the community's question.
- Open-source maintainer relationships and reproducible benchmark collaborations.
- Independent technical reviewers and guest contributors.
- Research citations, university labs and practitioner communities.

No unsolicited bulk messages, fake engagement, vote manipulation, fabricated testimonials or undisclosed paid promotion. Follow each community's self-promotion and link rules.

## Four flagship campaign pillars

| Pillar | Anchor content | Distribution asset |
|---|---|---|
| AI infrastructure economics | 70B VRAM, GPU memory and API-vs-self-hosted analysis | Calculator walkthrough, architecture diagram, cost-model thread |
| Secure AI infrastructure | Self-hosted LLM security | Threat-model checklist, hardening guide, practitioner discussion |
| Cloud and developer engineering | Local AI hardware and production AI operations | Build guide, reproducible configuration, short technical video |
| Startup infrastructure economics | AI SaaS cost and self-hosting decision | Unit-economics worksheet, founder memo, newsletter feature |

## Weekly cadence after editorial acceptance

- Monday: one evidence-led LinkedIn post tied to the week's article.
- Tuesday: short X thread with one chart or reproducible calculation.
- Wednesday: community contribution answering a relevant question; link only when useful and permitted.
- Thursday: short video or calculator demonstration.
- Friday: newsletter edition with three primary-source links and one original insight.

Cadence is a starting hypothesis, not a quota. Editorial quality and community fit take precedence.

## Measurement

After G8 consent and analytics controls are implemented, track:

- Qualified article visits and engaged reading.
- Returning readers and newsletter confirmed subscribers.
- Calculator usage and result shares, without collecting user inputs.
- Referral source, inbound architecture-review leads and qualified conversion.
- Contributor applications, research collaborations and citations.

Report cohort, time window, denominator and attribution method. Do not claim virality or growth from impressions alone. Paid distribution requires a separate budget approval and channel-specific compliance review.
